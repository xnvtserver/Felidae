# Felidae Code of Conduct

The Felidae community is made up of professionals, contributors, maintainers, researchers, users, and volunteers from different backgrounds and regions who work together across many areas of the project.

This diversity is one of our strengths. It also means that people may communicate differently, approach problems from different perspectives, or disagree on technical and non-technical matters.

This Code of Conduct establishes the standards we expect from everyone participating in the Felidae community.

It applies equally to maintainers, contributors, project leaders, mentors, users seeking help, and anyone participating in project-related spaces.

The goal of this document is not to provide an exhaustive list of prohibited behavior. Instead, it provides principles for maintaining a respectful, productive, and professional community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces managed by the **Felidae project** or **Xnovity Softwares**, including:

* GitHub repositories
* Issues and pull requests
* GitHub Discussions
* Documentation and project forums
* Mailing lists
* Community chats and communication channels
* Project meetings and events
* Contributor and maintainer interactions
* Other official communication spaces associated with Felidae

Behavior outside official project spaces may also be considered when it directly affects the safety, participation, or well-being of members of the Felidae community.

## Expected Behavior

### Be Friendly and Patient

Communicate respectfully and allow others reasonable time to understand, respond, investigate, or contribute.

People have different levels of experience, technical backgrounds, and familiarity with the project.

### Be Welcoming

We aim to maintain a community where people from different backgrounds can participate constructively.

Everyone should be treated with dignity and respect regardless of characteristics including, but not limited to:

* Race
* Ethnicity
* Nationality
* Culture
* Language
* Gender
* Gender identity or expression
* Sexual orientation
* Age
* Disability
* Religion
* Educational background
* Economic background
* Experience level
* Physical appearance
* Family status
* Political belief

Differences in background or identity must never be used as a reason to exclude, intimidate, insult, or harass another participant.

### Be Considerate

Your work may affect other contributors and users, just as your work may depend on theirs.

Consider the impact of technical decisions, communication, reviews, and changes on the wider project.

Felidae is an international project, and contributors may be communicating in a language that is not their first language. Minor communication mistakes should therefore be approached with patience and good faith.

### Be Respectful

Technical disagreements are normal and healthy when handled constructively.

Disagreement is not an excuse for hostility, personal attacks, insults, intimidation, or disrespectful behavior.

Focus criticism on:

* Code
* Architecture
* Design decisions
* Documentation
* Technical reasoning
* Observable behavior

Do not turn technical disagreements into personal conflicts.

### Communicate Professionally

Choose your words carefully and communicate in a professional manner.

Do not insult, demean, threaten, or deliberately embarrass other participants.

Harassment and exclusionary behavior are not acceptable.

Examples include, but are not limited to:

* Threats of violence
* Discriminatory jokes or language
* Personal attacks
* Racist, sexist, or otherwise discriminatory insults
* Unwelcome sexual attention
* Posting sexually explicit or violent material in inappropriate contexts
* Publishing or threatening to publish another person's private or identifying information without permission
* Persistent unwanted communication
* Deliberate intimidation
* Encouraging others to engage in harassment
* Repeatedly targeting another participant after being asked to stop

If someone reasonably asks you to stop behavior that is making them uncomfortable, stop.

## Handling Disagreements

Disagreements will occur in any active technical community.

When disagreements happen:

* Focus on the technical or project issue
* Assume good faith where reasonable
* Ask questions before making assumptions
* Support claims with evidence where possible
* Be open to changing your position
* Separate disagreement with an idea from judgment of the person proposing it
* Avoid escalating discussions unnecessarily

Different contributors may have different priorities, experiences, and perspectives.

The goal should be to understand the underlying reasoning and arrive at the best outcome for the project rather than to win an argument.

Mistakes are also a normal part of software development. When mistakes happen, focus on understanding the cause, correcting the issue, improving tests or processes, and preventing recurrence.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Behavior that is not acceptable in the Felidae community includes:

* Harassment
* Discrimination
* Threats
* Personal attacks
* Deliberate intimidation
* Doxing or disclosure of private information
* Repeated disruptive behavior
* Sexual harassment
* Persistent unwanted contact
* Deliberately sabotaging another contributor's work
* Knowingly misleading maintainers or contributors
* Abuse of project infrastructure or communication channels
* Retaliation against someone for reporting a Code of Conduct concern

## Reporting a Concern

If you believe someone has violated this Code of Conduct, please report the incident privately.

Contact:

**[info@xnovity.com](mailto:info@xnovity.com)**

When possible, include:

* A description of what happened
* Where the incident occurred
* Relevant dates or approximate times
* Links, screenshots, or other supporting information
* Names or usernames of the people involved
* Any additional context that may help with the review

Do not publicly post sensitive information when reporting a concern.

Reports will be reviewed as appropriately and confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action in response to behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, actions may include:

* A private warning
* Removal or moderation of inappropriate content
* Temporary restrictions on participation
* Closing or locking discussions
* Rejection of contributions
* Temporary suspension from project spaces
* Permanent removal from the Felidae community

Severe misconduct may result in immediate removal from project-controlled spaces.

Maintainers are expected to apply this Code of Conduct fairly and in good faith.

## No Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation is not acceptable.

Knowingly submitting false or malicious reports may itself be considered a violation of this Code of Conduct.
